How to format your file

One question per row. Column A is the question, column B is the right answer. Columns C, D, E are optional wrong choices. Rows with wrong choices become multiple choice; rows without them become typed answers.

Question | Answer | Wrong 1 | Wrong 2 | Wrong 3 Solve 2x + 5 = 17 | 6 | 11 | 8.5 | 12 Capital of Canada | Ottawa First U.S. president| George Washington;Washington

For typed answers, separate other accepted answers with a semicolon. Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter. From Word or Google Docs, paste lines like Q: … and A: ….
